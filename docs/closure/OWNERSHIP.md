# Closure Ownership

This matrix records the decision ownership required by the closure specification, Section 20.4. Named assignees are intentionally left as `TBD` until project governance assigns people or teams.

| Role | Owns | Assignee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research lead | North Star, protocol, Section 21, and claims | TBD |
| Lean systems owner | Extractor correctness and compatibility | TBD |
| Platform owner | Workspace, executor, artifacts, and CI | TBD |
| Data owner | Typed ledger, migration, and TPPR | TBD |
| Review-methods owner | Quorum, blinding, and adjudication | TBD |
| Security owner | Sandbox, seals, releases, and incidents | TBD |

One person may hold multiple ownership roles. Approvals for R4 protocol changes or security changes require a second reviewer.
